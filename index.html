Before the notary arrives

  • Every signer needs a valid photo ID, such as a driver's license, state ID or passport.
  • Don't sign beforehand. You sign in front of the notary.
  • Have every page ready, filled in except the signatures.
  • Everyone signing must be there in person.
  • Need witnesses? Wills, deeds and powers of attorney often do. Add them when you book.

Questions

How much does it cost?

Florida law limits the notary fee to $10 per notarization. The travel fee depends on where you are, and witnesses are priced per witness. We confirm the full price, itemized, before your appointment.

Do you provide witnesses?

Yes. We can bring one or two adult witnesses with your notary for wills, deeds and powers of attorney. Choose it when you book.

How fast can you come?

We answer requests 24/7, and same-day and after-hours visits are available when a notary is free.

Do you do online or remote notarization?

No. Every notarization is in person.

Do you handle loan or mortgage closings?

No, we don't handle loan or mortgage closings.
